Leaf webber/ roller and capsule borer:
Antigastra catalaunalis
Symptoms of damage
Webbing of terminal       leaves and bore the  tender shoots       at vegetative phase.
Flowers  and young capsules are bored at reproductive stage and the larvae feed on  developing seeds.
Identification of the pest
Larva
: Green in colour with black head and short white               hairs on the body
Adult
: Brown with orange brown forewings and  pale yellow            transparent hindwings
ETL : 10 larvae/ m2    at vegetative stage and 2 larvae / m2 at reproductive stage.
Management
Early sowing (first week of July) in
Kharif
season.
Intercrop with green gram, pearl millet (cumbu) and    groundnut.
Two sprays of Neem oil 2% or NSKE 5 % at 15 days    interval.
Spray Quinalphos 25 EC @  800 ml/ac.

Crop Protection :: Oil Seeds :: Pest of Sesamum
Leaf hopper:
Orosius albicinctus
Symptoms of damage:
Curling of leaf edges and leaves turn red or brown
Vector of sesamum
phyllody
Identification of the pest:
Adult
: Light brown coloured hopper
Management:
Intercropping of Sesamum + Redgram (6 : 1)
Seed treatment with  imidacloprid 600FS @ 7.5ml/kg seed.
Remove and destroy sesame plants with phyllody  symptom.
Remove alternate host
Cleome viscosa.
Spray Quinalphos 25 EC @ 800  ml/ac or thiamethoxam 25WG @ 40 g/ac or imidacloprid  17.8 SL @ 40 ml/ac.

Hawk moth or Dead head moth (Sphinx caterpillar):
Acherontia styx
Symptoms of damage:
Defoliation of plants.
Plant damage
Identification  of Insect Pest
Larva :
Stout, sturdy, greenish  with oblique stripes with a prominent dorsal curved anal horn.
Adult
Brownish giant hawk moth.
Thorax with a characteristic skull marking.
Abdomen has violet and yellow band.
Forewings are dark brown and hind wings are  yellowish with 2 black lines.
Management:
Deep ploughing exposes the pupae for predation to insectivorous birds.
Collection and destruction of caterpillars.
Spray  NSKE 5% or Quinalphos 25 EC 480 ml/ac

Aphids:
Aphis gossypii
Symptoms of damage:
Crinkling and curling of leaves
Leaves appear shiny and sticky due to honeydew excreted by the insects
Later sooty mold grows on honey dew and leaves have a black coating
Identification of the pest:
Adult:
Yellow to dark colored.
Management:
Seed treatment with imidacloprid 600FS @ 7.5 ml per kg seed.
Spray quinalphos 25 EC @ 800 ml/ac or thiamethoxam 25WG @ 40 g/ac or imidacloprid 17.8 SL @ 40 ml/ac.

Crop Protection :: Oil Seeds :: Pest of Sesamum
Gall fly,
Asphondylia sesami
Symptoms of damage:
Maggots feed on floral buds, leading to       withering and dropping of buds.
Formation of gall like  structure in buds and do not develop into capsules.
Pods  completely distorted without seeds.
Identification of the pest:
Maggot:
white and legless.
Adult
: Mosquito like fly
Management:
Intercrop with green gram, pearl millet and groundnut.
Clip the galls and burn the fallen buds.
Spray NSKE 5% or Neem oil 2%  at bud initiation stage.
