Onion Fly:
Delia antiqua
Symptoms of damage:
Onion leaves get rotten, shredded and rolled up
Growth of onion tubers gets hampered, tissues get decayed and becoming unfit for marketing
Identification of Pest:
Egg:
long whitish colour eggs are laid on the undersurface of onion near soil
Larva:
Whitish and adopous
Pupa:
Pupation takes place in soil
Adult:
Grey coloured fly
Management:
Adopt proper crop rotation
